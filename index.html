Questions owners usually ask first

Fair questions. Here are the plain answers.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive parts off their plate so they can spend time with customers. The person answering the phone is still the person answering the phone. They just aren't typing the same reminder text forty times a week.

Do I have to buy new software or learn a new system?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use, such as your email, calendar, and payment app. If something new is needed, it's typically one low-cost service, and we set it up and show you how it works.

What happens if something breaks?

Every project includes a support window, and we build in alerts so we usually know before you do. If you're on ongoing support, fixes are included. If you're not, you can call us and we'll sort it out at a fair hourly rate.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything runs under your accounts, and you receive a written summary of how it works. If you ever part ways with us, it keeps running and nothing is held hostage.

Is my customer information safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts. We don't copy customer lists to our systems, and we walk you through exactly what each automation can see and do before it goes live.
